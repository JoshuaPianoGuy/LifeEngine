WHAT TO REPORT
==============================================================================
  For P(viable): quote the CMH odds ratio, its 95% CI and p, and state the
  stratification ("stratified by map seed, k strata"). Quote the ICC from the
  mixed model beside it as the terrain variance component. Do NOT quote the
  pooled Fisher result except as the thing being corrected.

  For time-to-threshold: quote the Cox hazard ratio with the CLUSTER-ROBUST
  CI and p, and say the standard errors are clustered on map seed. Report the
  censoring fraction — runs that never reach the threshold are informative and
  the reader needs to know how many there were.

  For converged fitness: quote the mixed-model fixed effects and the ICC. The
  continuous outcome is the one worth leading with — thresholding a fitness
  trajectory into one viable/not bit discards most of what the run measured,
  and at these viability rates it is what forces the separation seen above.

  In every case, state the clustering in the Methods sentence, not just in a
  table footnote: "n = 100 runs per condition, 10 replicates on each of 10 map
  seeds; all tests treat the seed as the unit of independent replication."
